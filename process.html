Our Process

From first sketch to handover.

A straightforward five-step process, the same for every kitchen or wardrobe we build.

  1. Consultation

    We visit or meet at our showroom to understand your space, how you'll use it, and your budget.

  2. Design

    3D visualization and material selection — you see the finished kitchen or wardrobe before any work starts.

  3. Approval & Quotation

    A clear, itemized quotation for sign-off — no surprises once installation begins.

  4. Fabrication & Installation

    Built and installed by our team, with Hettich hardware throughout.

  5. Handover & After-Sales Support

    A final walkthrough, then ongoing support after you've moved in.
